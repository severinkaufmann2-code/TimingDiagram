Groups

A group is a titled block of channels, for example one per state of the machine. All groups share the one timeline and its transition points. The first group takes the channels that are already there. Click the title to rename it, drag the grip to reorder, fold a group away with the arrow. Drag a channel by its grip to move it into another group.

Phases

A phase is a titled stretch of time inside one group, drawn in the bar of that group. Its ends hold on to transition points and move with them. A folded group still shows its phases.

Comments

A comment is a numbered pin with a text. It can sit on a spot of a channel (5, 6, 8), on a transition point (2), on a phase (4), or next to the name of a channel (7), a group (3) or the diagram (1). The texts are in the Comments tab and in every export.

What stays as it is

A diagram without groups looks and works exactly as today, and files from version 1.0 open unchanged. “Add” now holds channel, transition point and group, because the toolbar has no room for more buttons on a small screen.

The ⋯ button of a group adds channels and phases to it, duplicates it, or removes it. “Duplicate” is the quick way to a second state with the same signals. Every step can be undone.

Add menu

One menu for everything that can be added. Channels and groups can also be added with the two buttons under the lanes, transition points with a click under the ruler, as before.

“Comment” in the toolbar (or the C key) picks up a pin. Click a spot in a lane, a transition point, a phase, or a name. Near a transition point the pin snaps to it and later moves with it. A pin can be dragged to another place at any time.
